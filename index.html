01 / 02 · INDUSTRY PROJECT

High Moisture Coal Handling Using Pneumatic System at Kamachi Industries

Duration: 1 monthTeam: 4 members
Aim
To enhance the high moisture coal handling and facilitate effective screening.
Problem
High-moisture coal clogged the wire-mesh filters of vibratory screens used in sponge-iron production.
Solution
Used compressed air available across the plant to remove clogging at high velocity using the Rotary Shoot Blower principle.

Duration: 6 monthsTeam: 6 members
Aim
To increase production in lathe machines in small-scale industries.
Solution
Developed an electronic Vernier-based measurement system with a diamond-tip probe, Arduino microcontroller and LCD display to show the real-time diameter of a rotating workpiece.
